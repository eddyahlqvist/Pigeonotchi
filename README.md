# Pigeonotchi 🐦

Pigeonotchi is a virtual pet game written in Python, inspired by the idea of Tamagotchi, but with pigeons!

The player takes care of a pigeon throughout its life, keeping it fed, healthy, and happy while watching it grow from an egg into adulthood and eventually old age.

## The Idea

Pigeons have needs that change as time passes. The player can interact with their pigeon, care for it, and influence how its life unfolds.

One of the main goals is to have a persistent world where time continues to matter even when the game isn't running. When the player returns, the game will simulate the time that has passed since their last visit.

Pigeons will go through different life stages, and eventually their lives will come to an end. Future generations may carry on their legacy.

## Development

Pigeonotchi is a personal hobby and learning project, built from scratch in Python.

The game will grow gradually as I experiment with new ideas, mechanics, and programming concepts. There is no fixed roadmap or deadline.

The goal is simply to learn, have fun, and create a little world full of pigeons.

*No pigeons were harmed during the writing of this README. Yet.*
